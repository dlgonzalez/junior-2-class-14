Part 3: Modals of Deduction

When we are guessing what an animal is, we use special verbs depending on how sure we are. Look at the Certainty Meter!

The Certainty Meter 📊

❌ 0% (NO)
🤔 50% (Maybe)
✅ 100% (YES)

CAN'T

"It's impossible."
(No wings? It can't be a bird).

MIGHT / MAY / COULD

"I'm not sure."
(It's brown... it could be a bear).

MUST

"I am 100% sure."
(It has spines! It must be a hedgehog).

Step 1: Choose the correct modal

Clue: It has webbed feet and a beak.

It be a duck.

Clue: It doesn't have fur or legs.

It be a bear.

Clue: It's flying, but it's very dark. I can't see.

It be a bat or a bird.

Clue: It is covered in fur and it's roaring.

It be a lion.

Clue: It isn't moving. (We don't know why).

It be scared.

Step 2: Right or Wrong?

"It has scales and swims. It can't be a fish."
"It's very tall and has a long neck. It must be a giraffe."
"It has 8 legs. It might be a spider."
"It doesn't have wings. It must be a bird."

Part 4: The Hot Seat Competition

📜 Rules & Demo

  1. One student (The Guesser) sits in a chair facing the class (back to the screen).
  2. The teacher clicks a card to reveal a secret animal on the screen.
  3. The class shouts clues about the animal's body parts.
  4. The Guesser uses Must or Can't to win a point for their team!
Class: "It has scales and sharp teeth! It swims fast!"
Guesser: "It can't be a bird... It must be a shark!"

💬 Cheat Sheet for the Class

  • 👉 "It has fur / scales / feathers."
  • 👉 "It doesn't have wings / paws."
  • 👉 "It can fly / swim / jump."
  • 👉 "It lives in the jungle / the water."

Part 5: Create a Creature & Dictation

Invent a totally new animal! Follow the 3 steps below to build your creature sentence by sentence. Do not write the name of your animal!

🛠️ Step-by-Step Monster Builder

1 Body Parts

What does it look like? Choose 3+ parts from the vocab list.

Use these phrases:

  • 👉 "My animal has scales and claws."
  • 👉 "It is green and very big."
  • 👉 "It doesn't have wings."

2 Abilities

What can it do? Where does it live?

Use these phrases:

  • 👉 "It can fly very high."
  • 👉 "It eats meat / leaves."
  • 👉 "It lives in the jungle."

3 Deduction

Give a big clue with a Modal (must/can't/might)!

Use these phrases:

  • 👉 "It must be dangerous."
  • 👉 "Because it has wings, it can't be a fish."
  • 👉 "It might be a reptile."
📝

Teacher's Complete Example:

"My animal has green scales, big webbed feet, and sharp teeth. It doesn't have fur. It can swim very fast and hide in rivers. Because it has scales and webbed feet, it must live in the water. It can't fly. It might be a crocodile!"
